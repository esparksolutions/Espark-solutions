What We Do

Our Services

From routine maintenance to advanced laboratory development — we cover the full spectrum of electrical engineering needs for industry, commerce, and homes.

🔧

Breakdown & Preventive Maintenance

Industrial breakdown response and scheduled preventive maintenance for compressors, overhead cranes, heaters, transformers, air conditioners, and AHU units — for homes and commercial premises.

🌿

Environmental Monitoring Services

Comprehensive site monitoring including sound levels, lux measurement, earth resistance testing, and air pollution measurement to ensure regulatory compliance and workplace safety.

📐

Calibration Services

Precision calibration for electrical instruments and measurement equipment, ensuring accuracy and traceability to national and international standards.

🏗️

Lab Development & Setup

End-to-end development of Electrical Test Labs, PD (Partial Discharge) Labs, Soundproof Rooms & Labs, and upgradation of transformer test laboratories — including brand new lab setups.

💻

Test Report Automation & Web Development

Custom web app development for test report automation and professional website development tailored for engineering organisations and institutions.

🔍

Electrical Inspections & Third-Party Support

Independent electrical inspections and inspection support services, deploying qualified engineers as certified third-party inspectors for industries and organisations.

🛡️

Electrical Safety Audits

Thorough electrical safety audits for manufacturing plants and commercial facilities, identifying risks and ensuring compliance with safety codes and standards.

📋

ISO & NABL Audit Support

External ISO audits, NABL IEC/ISO 17025 preparation and support, supplier auditor services, and vendor audits to help organisations achieve and maintain accreditation.

🏠

Home Electrical Automation

Smart automation solutions for home electrical appliances — enhancing energy efficiency, convenience, and safety for residential properties.
